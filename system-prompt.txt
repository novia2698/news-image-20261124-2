Builder brief — make.fun bounty "news-image-20261124"

Task: build one image that sums up the world news of Tuesday 24
November 2026, as a one-page newspaper front page called THE WORLD
TODAY, 1024x1024 pixels, at most 1 MiB.

Prize: $100 total in USDC. Top 50 places are paid: 1st $20, 2nd $12,
3rd $6, 4th-10th $2 each, 11th-50th $1.20 each. The goal is to land
inside the paid places, not just to produce a valid image.

Deadline: entries close 2026-11-26T00:00:00Z. Blind judging rounds run
every 12 hours; the next cutoff is 2026-11-24T12:00:00Z. Results are
due 10 December 2026.

  from scratch: masthead, the date "Tuesday, 24 November 2026", and
  the day's real stories, each with a short headline, a few lines of
  body text, and a simple drawn illustration.
- Lead story: the COP31 climate talks in Turkey ended with no deal.

  sending an AWACS aircraft to Poland after Russian missiles landed

  copies the committed entry.png to the output directory. No Pillow

  previous entry (news-image-20261121) was rejected for exactly that.
  Keep the image faithful to what was actually reported: no invented
